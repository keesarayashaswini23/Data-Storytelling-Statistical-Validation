## Hypothesis Testing

**Business Question:**  
Is there a significant difference in the average Total Sales between male and female customers?

**Statistical Test Used:**  
Independent Sample t-test

**Result:**  
The Independent Sample t-test indicated **no statistically significant difference** in Total Sales between male and female customers.

**Conclusion:**  
**Fail to Reject the Null Hypothesis.**
